04Deployment & hosting

Dedicated single-tenant (default, all tiers)

Your own isolated instance — not a shared multi-tenant environment. Hosted by us or in your own cloud account. Real isolation today, without waiting on the multi-tenant MSSP architecture still in development (see the RBAC, Multi-Tenancy & Governance document).

Self-hosted (Enterprise)

Run it entirely in your own environment; we provide support and updates against the SLA above without hosting it ourselves.

06Market position

Meaningfully below the incumbent's real spend

ServiceNow Vulnerability Response lists at $11,000–$24,500/fulfilled-user/year, with real benchmarked deployments running $40,000–$120,000/year plus a device-based component. VulnHunter's Professional tier — the direct feature-for-feature comparison point — lists at $38,000/year with a real SLA included, and Enterprise starts below ServiceNow's own high end while adding 24×7 support and a dedicated TAM.

07Launch readiness — what has to be true to sell this

The pricing and SLA above are the real, decided commercial model. Honoring the SLA table in §3 operationally requires standing up:

Support operations

A ticketing system, on-call rotation for Enterprise's 24×7 tier, and a documented escalation path — none of this exists yet as a staffed function.

Uptime monitoring & a status page

Real external uptime monitoring and a public status page are what make the uptime commitment in §3 measurable and credible to a paying customer — not yet built.
