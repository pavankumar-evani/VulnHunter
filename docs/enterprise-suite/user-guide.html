User & Operations Guide

Task-oriented "how do I...?" answers for every real workflow in the app — what's actually there, what isn't (yet), and where each control lives.

This searches the guide on this page. Inside the running app, Ask Quanta (/ask) searches this same content live, deterministically — see §07.

01Your account

How do I log in?/login

Email and password, checked against the real local user store. A "Sign in with SSO" button appears only when a real OIDC provider is configured server-side — otherwise it's simply absent, not a disabled stub.

How do I log out, and why two different messages?/logout

Log out from the account menu, /profile, or automatically after idle-timeout — all three sign you out first, then land you on /logout, a display-only confirmation. It shows "signed out after inactivity" or a generic "session ended" message depending on which of the two happened — the page itself never calls the logout API.

How do I change my password?/profile

One field: a new password, 8 characters minimum. No "confirm password" field, no "current password" required — submitting changes it immediately.

02Administration & RBAC

There's no separate "RBAC settings" page — role and team, set here, are the entire access-control model.

How do I add a new user?/admin

Admin-only. The Add User form takes email, name, a password (8+ characters), a role (user or admin — only two exist), and an optional team.

How do I change someone's role or team?/admin

Same page, "Team Management" table. Role is a dropdown per row that saves the moment you change it. Team is a free-text field with its own Save button — a blank team means that user's reads aren't filtered by team at all.

Where is RBAC actually configured?/admin

Right here. There's no granular permission matrix beyond role (admin/user) and team (which findings/approvals a non-admin's queries return). See the RBAC, Multi-Tenancy & Governance document for the full, honest model — including what team-scoping does and doesn't protect against.

03Managing assets & ownership

How do I assign a finding to a person or a team?/assignments

Open any finding and use the Ownership panel, or select rows on Assignments and choose Assign. Pick an owner, a team, or both: a finding can sit with a team until someone picks it up. Every assignment is written to the audit trail with who made it and when.

Bulk assignment handles up to 2,000 findings at once. "Auto-route from asset ownership" previews first, then fills only the gaps using each asset's owning team; findings that already have an owner are never overwritten.

How do I see who owns what, and where ownership is missing?/ownership

Ownership Analytics shows coverage (assigned, team-only, unowned), work status by team and by person, ageing of open work, and the most urgent unowned findings so gaps are visible before an SLA is missed. Owners move findings through Open, In progress, Blocked and Resolved; "Resolved" means the owner reports it fixed and the next scan confirms it.

How do I manage users and teams?/admin/people

Admins create teams with a manager and description, assign people to teams, and see each person's and team's live workload on one page. A team cannot be deleted while findings are assigned to it. Non-admin users see only their own team's findings and can update status only on findings assigned to them or their team.

How do I change an asset's owner, team, IP/MAC, or environment?/assets

Click "Edit" on that asset's row. The modal covers: Owner, Team, IP address, MAC address, Environment (Production/Staging/Dev/Unknown), and a remediation-schedule override. Each field saves independently on submit.

Bulk changes: use the "Import owner/team from a CMDB export" panel on the same page (CSV upload → column mapping → apply).

Where do I change an asset's internal/external-facing classification?/risk

Not in the Asset Inventory edit modal — it's a separate dropdown, inline in the table, on the Risk Management page. Always a manual classification; never inferred from a real network scan.

Can I change an asset's EOL/EOS status?

No — this one's worth being precise about. EOL/EOS is a read-only badge, computed server-side from the asset's OS string against a real vendor-lifecycle table. There's no edit field for it anywhere, deliberately: it's a derived fact, not something an owner should be able to override.

04Exceptions & approvals — two different decisions

How do I request an exception? Is there a separate approval step?/exceptions

Pick the finding, write a reason (candidate compensating controls are suggested by keyword — insert one with a click), set an expiry (quick +30/+90/+180-day buttons or a date picker).

"Approved by" is a plain text field filled in at request time — there is no separate in-app approval action for exceptions, despite what "request/approve" framing elsewhere might suggest. The only action available afterward is Revoke (admin-only, active exceptions only). Expiry is automatic.

How do I approve, reject, or trigger a remediation request?/remediation-approvals

This is the real human sign-off gate — a genuinely different decision from an exception ("proceed with the fix" vs. "accept the risk instead"). Findings whose policy calls for change management appear under "awaiting a request." Once requested, each row gets:

  • Approve — records who decided; runs a real, read-only AD group-membership check if one is configured
  • Reject — records who and why
  • Mark staging validated — an attestation field, not an automated staging check
  • Trigger Remediation (once approved) — confirm-gated; spends real API usage to generate the playbook

05Connecting your tools

How do I connect a scanner?/connections

Admin only. Add a connection, choose the source (Tenable, Qualys, Prisma Cloud, Cortex XSIAM, Infoblox, Axonius or Active Directory), enter its credentials, use Test connection, then set a schedule or click Sync now. Credentials are stored encrypted and never shown again. Each sync is queued as a job a worker runs, merges into the findings queue (a re-run updates rather than duplicates) and is recorded in the activity log; a second click on Sync now does not queue a second sync. If your policy keeps scanner credentials out of other systems, have the scanner push to Quanta with an API key instead (see below).

How do I open ServiceNow or Jira tickets automatically for urgent findings?/connections

Add a ServiceNow, Jira Cloud or Splunk connection (these are "push" connections) and set its rule: send findings at or above a severity, only those on the CISA KEV list, a minimum EPSS, and the most tickets per run (so a first sync cannot flood the service desk). Each run opens a ticket for matching findings not yet sent, most urgent first, and never creates a duplicate on a re-run. Ticket state is read back each sync.

How do I create an API key for a scanner, SOAR playbook or CI job?/connections

In the "Send data to Quanta" section, click Create an API key. Give it a name, tick only the access it needs (scopes: ingest:write send findings and scanner files in; tickets:update report ticket status; read:findings read findings out; controls:write report observed security controls; ai-usage:write report AI usage; soc:write send SIEM or XDR alerts) and choose an expiry (never, 30, 90 or 365 days). The key is shown once, so copy it immediately; Quanta keeps only a hash. Use it as Authorization: Bearer <key>. The key table lists your keys, and Revoke stops one working immediately. See docs/INTEGRATION_API.md for the endpoints.

How do I import a scanner CSV?/connections

Use "Import a scanner file" in the "Send data to Quanta" section: choose a CSV in the Tenable column layout, enter a source name, and upload. Tick "this is the complete export" only when the file is everything that scanner currently reports; it then removes that source's findings missing from the file, which is how fixed vulnerabilities leave the queue.

How do I see which ticket belongs to a finding?/queue

Open the finding (click its ID anywhere). The "External tickets" section lists tickets a push connection opened for it: reference, system, state (Open, In progress, Blocked or Resolved) and the last error if sending failed. State flows back on each sync and updates the finding's assignment status. A resolved ticket marks the assignment resolved but does not close the finding; the next scan decides that.

06Reports & alerts

How do I generate or schedule a report?/reports

Pick a period and generate an on-demand snapshot (viewable or downloadable as standalone HTML), or use the "Schedule automatic email reports" panel on the same page for recurring delivery.

How do I configure team alerts or send a test email?/notification-settings

The fuller config surface: report-schedule config (same as above), team alert-subscription rules (critical findings, zero-days, KEV/EPSS matches), a Preview → confirm-gated Send-Test flow, and a "Run checks now" button that runs the same due-check logic the background scheduler runs hourly. Real sending requires SMTP to be configured server-side.

07AI, search & analytics

There is no chatbot anywhere in this app

Two real, distinct AI-adjacent features exist — neither is a persistent conversational chat interface, and neither pretends to be.

How do I use AI Assist?/ai-assist

Pick a finding and an action (explain in plain English / draft remediation steps / write an executive summary), then confirm. This is the one feature that calls the real Claude API and spends real usage — unconfirmed, you get a free preview of the exact prompt.

How do I use Ask Quanta?/ask

Type a plain-English question. It deterministically matches a finding ID, a CVE, a count, or an asset name — or, failing that, matches this guide's FAQ content by keyword overlap. Explicitly not an LLM: it can never hallucinate an answer, because "no match" is always an option.

What does ML Insights actually compute?/ml-insights

Two real, unsupervised scikit-learn techniques over live data, no login required: an IsolationForest flags anomalous assets with a human-readable reason, and KMeans clusters findings you can drill into by severity/CVSS/EPSS. Deliberately no supervised prediction — nothing here forecasts what will get exploited.

08Controls, risk, hunting & AI usage

How do I read the guidance on a finding, and does it change by scan type?/queue

Open the finding (click its ID anywhere) and read "How to fix this". Guidance is chosen from the finding's scan type and its CWE or asset type: code flaws found by static analysis (SAST) or by testing a running app (DAST), vulnerable libraries (SCA, with the fixed version and upgrade command when known), committed secrets, infrastructure-as-code, container images, CI/CD pipeline weaknesses, test-coverage gaps in security-relevant files, and infrastructure hosts each get steps suited to that kind of problem. It says what it matched on; a finding with no specific entry gets a general approach, labelled as general. Further down, "Compensating controls for your environment" lists the MITRE ATT&CK mitigations for the techniques the flaw enables and whether each is in place on that asset. The guidance changes nothing by itself.

How do I bring in SARIF, pipeline or coverage results from my CI?/connections

Create an API key with the ingest:write scope (Connections, "Send data to Quanta"), then post the file from your pipeline: SARIF 2.1.0 from Semgrep, CodeQL, ZAP, Trivy, Checkov, gitleaks and similar tools to POST /api/ingest/sarif; a Cobertura, JaCoCo or lcov report to POST /api/ingest/coverage (only security-relevant files become findings). For GitHub Actions, GitLab CI and Jenkinsfile weaknesses, an operator runs quanta-admin scan-pipelines. Each finding carries a scan type (the newer ones are container, cicd and coverage); an explicit scan type sent with the data wins over inference. Examples are in docs/INTEGRATION_API.md.

How do I record a security control on an asset?/controls

Admin only. On Security Controls, under "Add a control", enter the asset name or a pattern such as WEB-*, choose the kind of control (for example EDR, network filtering, MFA), describe it in your own words and click Add control. To load many at once, use "or import a CSV". A control you or a CSV record is claimed; one an EDR or firewall integration reports through the controls API (an API key with the controls:write scope) is verified. Re-sending the same control refreshes its last-seen time instead of duplicating it. The more complete this list, the more specific the compensating-control advice and threat-model residual risk become; with nothing recorded for an asset, Quanta says unknown.

What do verified, claimed, absent and unknown mean in the compensating-control advice?/queue

In place (verified): a connector or script observed the control on that asset. In place (recorded, not verified): a person recorded it. Not in place: controls are recorded for the asset but this mitigation is not among them. Not known for this asset: nothing is recorded, so Quanta does not guess. The coverage percentage is indicative (verified counts 1, recorded counts 0.5), and compensating controls reduce risk; they do not close the finding.

How do I build a threat model and review its threats?/threat-models

Admin only. Under "New model", name it and start from an editable example, from your assets (components drafted from the findings), or empty. Open the model, adjust components, data flows and trust zones in the JSON editor (properties you leave out count as not recorded) and click Save model. Quanta raises STRIDE threats from explicit rules, not an LLM, and recomputes them whenever the model changes, so nothing goes stale. Each threat shows likelihood x impact, the live findings on the affected assets (KEV flagged), the mitigating controls recorded for them and the residual score. Use the Decision list to mark a threat accepted, mitigated or not-applicable; only those decisions are stored. Threats marked unconfirmed are questions to answer, not established weaknesses. The scores rank work for discussion; they are not a measurement.

How do I import a framework and collect evidence?/grc

Admin only. On Risk & Compliance, open the Controls tab. The built-in catalogs (NIST 800-53 r5, CSF 2.0 and an AI-governance set) are subsets. To work against every control, use "Import a full catalog": give it an id and upload the framework's OSCAL JSON (for a licensed standard, your licensed copy in OSCAL form). Then open the Evidence tab and click Collect evidence now; it also runs about once a day. Each automated test reports pass, fail, warn or na with what it measured and the threshold; with too little data it says na, never a pass. Back on the Controls tab each control shows its status and tests, and you can download the OSCAL assessment results. Quanta supplies evidence and workflow; it does not certify compliance, and a passing test shows that Quanta observed something, not that an auditor would agree.

How do I attest to a control Quanta cannot observe?/grc

On the Controls tab of Risk & Compliance, click Attest beside the control, say whether it is effective, partially-effective or ineffective, and give the statement it rests on (required). The attestation sits beside the automated evidence rather than replacing it, and shows as expired when it is no longer current.

How do I register a risk?/grc

Admin only. Open the Risk register tab. "Worth registering" lists suggestions drawn from live findings and threat models: click one to add it. Or fill in "Add a risk": title, owner, likelihood and impact from 1 to 5, a treatment (mitigate, accept, transfer, avoid) with the plan or reason, and a review date. The register shows inherent and residual level, owner, status and whether a review is overdue. Policies are on their own tab; anyone signed in can read the active ones and click "I have read this" to acknowledge.

How is the app organised, and where do I find a connector?/capabilities

The sidebar has eight numbered modules: Threat Detection & Response (SOC, alert triage, hunting, detection engineering, threat intelligence, dark web watch, SOAR), Application Security, DevSecOps & Supply Chain, Infrastructure & Exposure, AI Security, Remediation & Workflow, Risk, Governance & Compliance, and Administration. The sidebar shows one module at a time: pick a module and only its pages show; use Switch module or All modules to change, and the app remembers your choice. Each module ends with a Connectors list (the scanners, SIEM, ticketing system or Git host that feeds it). Open All modules under Home for a landing page per module that shows what it holds right now and what to connect when it is empty. Credentials for every connector are managed on Connections under Administration, and Ctrl/Cmd+K jumps to any page by name.

How do I build an API inventory and check it for OWASP API Top 10 problems?/api-security

Admin only. Quanta cannot watch traffic, so give it what you have. On Import, upload an OpenAPI or Swagger file (or fetch it from a URL; you are asked to confirm) and an access-log export from your gateway, load balancer or WAF, or have your log shipper push records with an API key that has the api:write scope. Inventory then lists every endpoint with its exposure, authentication, owner and the data it returns; endpoints in traffic that no spec describes are shadow endpoints. Import your own data-classification framework on Data classes so sensitivity follows your definitions; until then data is shown as unclassified. Findings lists OWASP API Top 10 indicators, each with an evidence chain and a cURL request a developer can run against a system they are allowed to test to confirm a true or false positive; Publish sends them to the main queue.

How do I send a protection policy to my WAF, and what does Quanta change?/api-security

Quanta changes nothing. On Protection policies create a policy (rate limit, bad sources, data-loss limit, geography or signature); it starts in monitor mode. Use Rules to review for an AWS WAF or Cloud Armor rule you apply yourself, or add a connection of type API protection policy endpoint on Connections and choose Send to my endpoint: Quanta posts a signed request to a URL you own and your automation decides. A blocking policy must be approved by a second administrator first. Your automation can report applied or rejected back, and each result raises an alert.

How do I run API security tests in my pipeline and fail the build?/api-security

On CI and rollout copy the GitHub Actions or GitLab CI example. Your tester posts its results to /api/ingest/api-test-results; the response says whether the gate passed, and the job fails when it did not. Start with report_only=true, then make the gate blocking.

How do I start a hunt from a proposal and close it with an outcome?/hunting

Admin only. Quanta is not a SIEM and does not run queries; it proposes where to look and you run the queries in your own tool. On Proposed hunts, each card is an open CVE that is on the KEV list or has an EPSS of 0.5 or more, with the hosts, the ATT&CK techniques Quanta tags and ready-made queries (Splunk SPL, to be adapted to your data model). Click Start this hunt, then on Hunts open it, run each query in your SIEM, and set each query's result (hits, no-hits, not-run, error). Add notes and follow-ups, tick "This hunt led to a new detection" if it did, set the status to closed and choose an outcome (confirmed, not-found or needs-data). Closing requires an outcome. The Overview tab then shows hunts closed and confirmed and how many of the techniques in your open findings have been hunted.

How do I triage a SOC alert?/hunting

Alerts reach Quanta from your SIEM or XDR through POST /api/ingest/alerts with an API key that has the soc:write scope. On the Alert triage tab they are ranked by a priority that reflects what Quanta knows about the host: its owner, open findings, and known-exploited ones, with the reasons listed. Open an alert to see findings that match it and a runbook (chosen by technique, then title keywords, else a generic one), and set status, disposition (true-positive, benign, false-positive, needs-data), assignee and notes. Quanta shows the runbook steps; responding stays with your team or SOAR.

How do I set an AI budget, and see which AI tools nobody reviewed?/ai-usage

Admin only. AI Usage shows tokens and spend by team, application, model and source. Quanta stores counts only, never prompts or responses, and the page lists which sources are reporting; cost is reported or estimated, and requests of unknown cost are counted separately, never shown as zero. To set a budget, use the "Add budget" form: choose the organization, a team or an application, a period (month, week, day), a limit in dollars and/or tokens, and a warning percentage. The table shows used, on-track-for and a state of ok, alert or exceeded. To find unreviewed tools, upload a proxy or DNS export under "AI applications found" (CSV with a domain column, optionally user and count); known AI services appear as unreviewed, which you can change to sanctioned or blocked. That status is a record for reporting; Quanta does not block anything.

How do I feed AI usage into Quanta?/connections

Three ways. Add an Anthropic usage or OpenAI usage connection on Connections, using that provider's Admin key. Or post events from a gateway or script to POST /api/ingest/ai-usage, or send OpenTelemetry JSON to /api/ingest/otlp/v1/traces, with an API key that has the ai-usage:write scope. Quanta's own calls are included. See docs/INTEGRATION_API.md. As with every connector here, these are built against public documentation and tested against mocked responses, not run against a live provider account.

09Applications, SBOMs & fix pull requests

How do I register an application?/applications

Admin only. On Applications & SBOM, add an application and fill in what you know: environment, platform, owner and team, business criticality, whether it is internet-facing, and (if you want fix pull requests) its Git provider, repository, default branch and the paths of its dependency files. The criticality and internet-facing flag feed the ranking, so set them honestly; they are your statement, not something Quanta discovers. Anyone signed in can view the list.

How do I upload or generate an SBOM for an application?/applications

Admin only. Open the application's Context & SBOM tab. To upload, choose a CycloneDX or SPDX JSON file; each application keeps one current SBOM. To generate one, give Quanta a requirements.txt, package.json, package-lock.json, pom.xml or go.mod; only a lock file gives transitive dependencies, and Quanta says so when you generate from a manifest alone. It never runs a package manager. Your CI can also upload an SBOM with an API key that has the ingest:write scope (POST /api/ingest/sbom). Quanta ships no advisory database: components show as vulnerable only when a scanner finding names the package or when you run the OSV check, which an admin confirms and which sends package URLs only. Without either, an SBOM shows components, not a clean bill of health.

How do I read the dependency and exposure graph and the ranked work?/applications

The graph shows the application's dependency tree (direct and transitive, with the blast radius of a vulnerable package) and, above it, the lane from the internet through any WAF, load balancer, DMZ and firewall that you recorded in the network topology file. With nothing recorded the lane is absent, not guessed. The ranked work lists every finding with a score breakdown: CVSS, EPSS, KEV, application criticality, package sensitivity, attack surface, position in an attack chain, and a lower multiplier when the network path is denied. Dependency findings are grouped into the one upgrade that closes them. The weights are a disclosed, editable choice, not a standard, so use the order to sequence work, not as a measurement.

How do I propose and open a fix pull request?/fix-prs

Admin only for anything that changes state. First store a GitHub or GitLab connection on Connections (an access token) and set the repository on the application. Then create a proposal from the ranked work: a dependency upgrade, or a code fix for a first-party finding. Review the diff and evidence on Fix Pull Requests and Approve it. Opening is a dry run showing the branch, files and description until you tick confirm; a real open creates a new branch and a pull request. Quanta never merges, never writes to the default or a protected branch, and refuses pipeline files, CODEOWNERS, keys and env files. It does not run a package manager or tests, so refresh the lock file and run your tests on the branch; when a lock file exists the pull request opens as a draft. Use Sync to read the state back, and Verify after the next scan. The connectors were built to public documentation and tested against fakes, not run against a live host: try a trial repository first.

How do I keep pull request status up to date automatically?/fix-prs

An hourly scheduler tick follows every open fix pull request and re-checks merged ones against the latest scan. It does nothing when no pull request is open, and it is on by default; set QUANTA_GITOPS_SYNC to false, 0 or no to turn it off. You can also set up the webhook (next entry) so the Git host tells Quanta as soon as the state changes. The Fix Pull Requests page shows whether the hourly check and the webhook are active. Verification is evidence from the next scan, not proof.

How do I set up the GitHub or GitLab webhook?/api/inbound/git-webhook

Set a shared secret in QUANTA_GIT_WEBHOOK_SECRET (or QUANTA_GIT_WEBHOOK_SECRET_FILE for a mounted secret), then add a webhook in the repository settings that sends pull request events to POST /api/inbound/git-webhook with the same secret. GitHub signs the body (X-Hub-Signature-256, HMAC-SHA256); GitLab sends the secret as X-Gitlab-Token. With no secret set, Quanta refuses every delivery (503); a bad signature gets 401; a pull request Quanta did not open is ignored. Only the pull request URL and its open, merged or closed state are used. No login or API key is involved, because the signature is the check. Tested against hand-signed payloads; no delivery has been received from a live host.

What do I do after a merge to get verification sooner?/fix-prs

On the merged proposal, choose "Queue a rescan on the scanner connections" (admin). It queues a sync on each enabled scanner pull connection, meaning those whose output is findings. If none is configured it tells you to upload the next scan instead. Once that scan lands, Verify reads it and marks the fix verified, still present or awaiting a rescan.

Why did my dependency pull request open as a draft?/fix-prs

The repository has a lock file, and Quanta never runs a package manager, so it cannot refresh it for the new version. The proposal page shows a callout saying so: refresh the lock file on the branch, run your tests, and mark the pull request ready when checks pass.

How do I load demo data for the application pages?quanta_admin.py

Run python cli/quanta_admin.py seed-appsec-demo (optionally --name NAME). It registers a demo application from the sample SBOM plus five findings under the source appsec-demo, and prints the network_topology.yaml entry to add if you want the exposure lane to show. --remove undoes exactly those. It is sample data, not a scan result.

How do I use the release gate in CI?/pipeline-gates

Create an API key with the read:findings scope (Connections, "Send data to Quanta"). On Pipeline Gates, open "Add it to a pipeline", pick GitHub Actions, GitLab CI or shell, and paste the snippet; it calls GET /api/gate/evaluate for your application and fails the job when the result says block. "Try it" runs an evaluation on demand, and the Policy section shows what is set to block, warn or off (edit remediation/config/pipeline_gates.yaml). A finding with an approved exception is not counted. Every evaluation is recorded under History.

How do I use the secure design assistant?/secure-design

Open Secure Design and answer the questionnaire about the feature or system you are planning. You get security requirements with OWASP ASVS references, the pipeline controls to put in place and questions for the threat model. They come from explicit rules (design_rules.yaml), not from a model, so the same answers give the same result. Treat the output as a starting checklist for your design review.

How do I add our own DevSecOps control?/devsecops

Admin only. On DevSecOps, open the "Our own controls" tab and add a control that is specific to your organisation. It uses the same evidence model as the built-in library, so it appears beside the built-in controls with its own evidence status. You can edit or delete it later.

10Getting help

How do I get step-by-step instructions to fix a finding?/queue

Click the finding's ID anywhere and open "How to fix this": a summary, why it matters, ordered steps, how to confirm it is fixed, effort and references. It says what it matched on and is tailored from what Quanta knows (fixed version and upgrade command, Windows KB number, CISA KEV listing, EPSS, breached SLA, the scanner's own recommendation). On /quanta-scan, use the "How to fix" button in the Guidance column. It changes nothing itself, and states plainly what Quanta can automate for that finding, if anything.

What does Quanta recommend if I cannot fix a finding right now?/queue

The same section lists compensating controls for that class of issue. If your firewall and EDR coverage is recorded in security_controls.yaml (it ships empty), it shows your actual coverage; otherwise it says the controls are general. The guidance is curated for common classes, not every CWE; an unmatched finding gets a generic approach, labelled generic. Compensating controls reduce risk but do not close the finding.

How do I request a new feature?/support

Open a ticket from Support and choose "A feature request". It is stored in your own deployment's database and triaged by your administrators; nothing goes to a public tracker.

How do I check if Quanta is running and healthy?

There's no dedicated status page in the UI, but there's a real, honest answer. The simplest check: if the Overview page's KPIs load and keep refreshing every 20 seconds without an error, the API and backend are up — a genuine signal, not a guess. For a definitive, scriptable check (worth doing before filing a bug), call /api/status directly — it returns real, live-checked facts (overall status, app version, process uptime, live finding/playbook counts, and whether the background notification scheduler is alive), not a hardcoded "OK". No login is required, unless your deployment has turned on the stricter login-for-reads setting.

How do I report a bug, or a real security issue?/support

Bugs/unexpected behavior: open a ticket from Support with the exact command, full error output, and environment, and say who is affected and how urgent it is. The ticket is prioritised (P1 to P4), routed to a team queue and tracked against response and resolution targets. Team members work the queue, admins see all of it, and the Analytics tab shows SLA compliance, response and resolution times, and backlog by team. A real security issue in Quanta itself: the private contact in SECURITY.md — never a public issue.

What's the fastest way to find an answer myself?/ask

Try Ask Quanta first — it searches this exact guide's content live, with no page reload and no cost. This page's own search box above does the same search, offline.
